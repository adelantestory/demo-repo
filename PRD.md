# Product Requirements Document (PRD): "Meetup for Pet-Friendly Locations" Landing Page

## Overview
Create a quick, simple prototype landing page for a "Meetup" platform targeting pet owners seeking pet-friendly locations. The landing page should capture interest, encourage sign-ups, and briefly describe the concept. No backend integration required; static HTML/CSS/JS only.

## Goals
- Attract pet owners interested in discovering and sharing pet-friendly spots.
- Collect user interest via an email sign-up form.
- Communicate the core value proposition clearly and visually.

## Features

### 1. Hero Section
- Catchy headline (e.g., "Find Pet-Friendly Places & Meet Other Pet Owners!")
- Supporting tagline.
- Eye-catching hero image or illustration (pets at a park, café, etc.).

### 2. Value Proposition
- 2-3 bullet points or icons describing key benefits:
  - Discover local pet-friendly locations (parks, cafés, shops).
  - Connect with other pet owners.
  - Share your favorite spots & experiences.

### 3. Email Sign-Up Form
- Simple form: Name (optional), Email (required).
- "Get Notified" or "Join the Community" button.
- Success message on submission (no actual backend, just JS feedback).

### 4. Pet-Friendly Locations Showcase
- Carousel or grid with 3-5 example locations (image + name + short description).
- Static examples (e.g., "Central Bark Park", "Paws Café").

### 5. Footer
- Social media icons (static links or placeholders).
- Privacy notice/disclaimer.

## Design & Style
- Friendly, welcoming, colorful.
- Use pet-themed illustrations or icons.
- Responsive design (mobile/tablet/desktop).
- Modern, clean layout; avoid clutter.

## Technical Requirements
- HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (no frameworks, just vanilla).
- All assets (images/icons) should be free to use or placeholders.
- Self-contained; no external dependencies except optionally Google Fonts or open-source icon sets.

## Non-Goals
- No login/authentication.
- No backend/database.
- No location services or real-time features.

## Success Criteria
- The page communicates the main idea effectively.
- Users can submit their email and see confirmation.
- Visuals and copy are engaging for pet owners.
- Works well on desktop and mobile.

## Next Steps
